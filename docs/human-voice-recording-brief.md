# Mind Nav human voice recording brief

## Direction

Record one original Black British female voice for Mind Nav. The performance
should feel warm, grounded, conversational, emotionally alert, and close-mic'd—
like a trusted person sharing the room, never like an announcer, cartoon, or
automated assistant.

Use natural pace and breath. Keep the energy responsive rather than uniformly
calm. Record two or three subtly different takes of short acknowledgements so
the app can vary them without sounding repetitive.

## Usage and delivery

- Grant commercial mobile-app use for iOS and Android, including updates.
- Grant the right to edit, master, compress, and bundle the recordings.
- Do not grant or request voice-cloning, model-training, or synthetic derivative
  rights.
- Deliver dry 48 kHz, 24-bit mono WAV files with no music, reverb, or effects.
- Leave roughly 150 ms of room tone at each end for clean editing.
- Name each file with its phrase ID, for example `ack_here_01.wav`.

## Core presence cues

- `ack_here`: "I'm here."
- `ack_listening`: "I'm listening."
- `ack_go_on`: "Go on."
- `ack_take_time`: "Take your time."
- `ack_got_you`: "I've got you."
- `ack_thank_you`: "Thank you for telling me."
- `ack_pause`: "We can pause here."
- `ack_interrupt`: "You can interrupt me whenever you need to."
- `prompt_more`: "Would you like to say a little more?"
- `prompt_body`: "What are you noticing in your body?"
- `prompt_next`: "What would feel like one kind next step?"
- `prompt_choice`: "Would you like reflection, a practical tool, or just some space?"

## Guided regulation cues

- `ground_begin`: "Let's find one steady point together."
- `ground_feet`: "Notice where your body meets the surface beneath you."
- `breath_in`: "Breathe in, gently."
- `breath_out`: "And let the breath out without forcing it."
- `look_around`: "Let your eyes find something neutral or pleasant nearby."
- `return_ready`: "Come back when you're ready."
- `complete`: "That's enough for now."

## Safety cues

- `safety_pause`: "Let's pause the usual guidance for a moment."
- `safety_choice`: "You stay in control of what happens next."
- `safety_support`: "If you may be in immediate danger, call emergency services now. In the United States, you can also call or text 988."

Safety wording remains policy-controlled in the app. Recordings support the
screen but never claim that a member is safe and never trigger an external
contact.
